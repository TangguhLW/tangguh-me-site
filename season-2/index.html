01 / The summons

The last knight.

A kingdom has forgotten the sunrise. One letter still remembers the way.

Follow the seal into the story.

02 / The ancient forest

Where the light waits.

Beyond the sleeping oaks, the towers are still standing. Keep walking.

The path leads to the northern gate.

03 / The northern gate

No guards. No bells.

Only the bridge answers your footsteps. The kingdom has been waiting.

Pass beneath the stone arch.

04 / The silent hall

An empty crown.

The king is gone. Beneath his throne, a stair descends into the old world.

Follow the light beneath the throne.

05 / The dragon crypt

The keeper of dawn.

The dragon never stole the sun. It kept the last ember alive.

Carry the ember to the highest tower.

06 / The first morning

And the world remembers.

A little light was enough. Somewhere below, the bells begin again.

Every ending leaves a door open.
